About the artist

Looking closely is where it starts.

I’m Shamalka, a self-taught artist from Sri Lanka, now living in Australia. My love for art began in middle school, but life led me down a different path, and I became an engineer. Years later, through looking inward and searching for my life’s purpose, I found my way back to the passion that had always been a part of me.

For me, art is a way of expressing what words cannot. Through my work, I hope to share the beauty I see in people, animals, and nature. Even in the little things we often overlook. More than anything, I want my art to touch hearts, bring comfort, and help people see the world with a little more wonder. As you explore my art, I hope you find a little piece of yourself here, too.

Welcome to my world. I’m glad you’re here.

How your details are used

  • Forms are delivered to the artist’s email through the form service FormSubmit. Nothing you send is published on this website.
